Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 September 2026

SHP Technology ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Office Canteen application. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within this specific installation of the software.

1. Employee Data Collected

The application stores basic employee information necessary for canteen operations. This includes Employee Names, Departments, and WhatsApp Numbers. This data is entered manually by the canteen operator.

2. WhatsApp Number Usage

WhatsApp numbers are stored strictly to generate click-to-chat links. The application does not share these numbers with any third-party APIs, nor does it send messages in the background. The communication remains entirely between the canteen operator's device and WhatsApp.

3. Billing and Account Data

The system records daily food consumption, billing totals, and payment histories to maintain accurate ledgers. This financial data is stored locally in the application's database.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in the local SQLite database configured during deployment. The canteen administration is responsible for securing the hosting environment and performing regular database backups. SHP Technology does not have remote access to your database.

5. Operator Responsibility

The organization deploying this software is the data controller. It is your responsibility to ensure that employees consent to having their names and numbers stored for billing purposes.
